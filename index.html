🧭 How It Works

From idea to adventure
in minutes.

Designed for real travellers — solo, with friends, or on official duty.

1
Create Your Profile

Register with email, Google, or Apple. Set travel interests and preferred modes. Verification unlocks all features.

2
Plan Your Trip

Build a day-wise itinerary, set your budget, choose travel mode, and share with buddies or publish publicly.

3
Find Travel Buddies

Search verified travellers going to the same destination. Chat unlocks only after mutual acceptance for safety.

4
Travel with Confidence

Use real-time nearby utilities, SOS emergency support, and the local authority directory — wherever you go.
